# Camera

Takes a set number of photos as fast as the hardware allows and returns them as files, with no preview or shutter button. Use it when your app captures frames alongside a sensor reading or other event. Asks for camera permission the first time it runs. Phones only.

**When to use it.** Reach for it when your app needs the camera in code: a burst of photos matched to a sensor reading, a held-open session with controls and recording, or a live preview you draw yourself. For a picker where the person chooses a photo, use the platform file picker instead.
